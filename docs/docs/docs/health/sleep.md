# Why Is Sleep Important for Your Health and Mood?

Sleep is more than time spent resting. During sleep, the body and brain carry out important recovery processes.

## Sleep and daily energy

A consistent sleep routine can help you feel more alert and ready for the day. Poor sleep may leave you tired and less able to concentrate.

## Sleep and mood

Lack of quality sleep can make it harder to manage emotions and cope with everyday stress. Good sleep is one part of maintaining overall well-being.

## Simple sleep habits

Try keeping a consistent bedtime, reducing screen use before bed, creating a comfortable sleep environment, and limiting caffeine late in the day if it affects your sleep.

If sleep problems persist or significantly affect daily life, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.
